YOUR CLEANING CHECKLIST

Essential Clean

Everyday upkeep

Essential+ replaces only the selected room group with the detailed checklist.Selected rooms receive the Essential+ checklist; all other rooms remain Essential.

All Rooms

  • Beds made
  • Reachable vents & cobwebs dusted
  • Mirrors & glass polished
  • Reachable surfaces wiped
  • Furniture & electronics dusted
  • Trash bins emptied & relined
  • Floors swept, mopped or vacuumed

Kitchen

  • Load dishes into dishwasher
  • Range hood wiped
  • Stovetop cleaned
  • Appliance exteriors wiped
  • Microwave interior & exterior cleaned
  • Countertops & backsplash wiped
  • Cabinet exteriors wiped
  • Sink & faucet polished

Bathrooms

  • Tub & shower scrubbed
  • Toilets cleaned & sanitized
  • Cabinet exteriors wiped
  • Countertops wiped
  • Sink & faucet polished

Available additions

Optional work · selected and quoted separately

  • Interior oven
  • Interior fridge
  • Interior cabinets
  • Interior windows
  • Blinds
  • Baseboards
  • Patio / balcony
  • Garage sweeping

Essential+ focuses on one area per visit: kitchen OR bathrooms. Additional requests are confirmed during quoting.
